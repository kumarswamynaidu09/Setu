# Ethical Field Validation & User Research Plan — SETU

## 1. Research Objectives
To evaluate the pedagogical usability, assistive efficacy, and operational feasibility of SETU in mainstream Indian government secondary schools (Classes 5 to 10).

---

## 2. Target Stakeholder Cohorts

### Cohort A: Mainstream Class Teachers (n = 12)
- **Role:** Subject teachers (Science, Mathematics, Social Studies).
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Time to launch classroom session, ease of live speech captioning, usability of live caption correction, perception of additional workload.

### Cohort B: Special Educators & Resource Teachers (n = 8)
- **Role:** Itinerant special educators and school resource room coordinators.
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Pedagogical appropriateness of diagram audio descriptions, relevance of AAC communication phrase presets, suitability of simplified text versions.

### Cohort C: Students with Diverse Sensory & Learning Needs (n = 30)
- **Profiles:** Students with mild-to-moderate hearing loss, low vision, reading/learning difficulties (dyslexia), or speech reluctance.
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Speed of joining via 6-character room code, ease of switching between Read / Listen / Respond / Follow modes, clarity of text-to-speech, comfort with AAC cards.

### Cohort D: School Accessibility Coordinators / Headmasters (n = 4)
- **Role:** School principals and ICT coordinators.
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Ease of server deployment, system uptime, operational telemetry usefulness, zero-privacy burden.

---

## 3. Ethical Safeguards & Consent Protocol for Minors
1. **Institutional Authorization:** Formal Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the District Educational Officer (DEO) and School Management Committee (SMC).
2. **Guardian Informed Consent:** Prior written consent provided in regional languages (Telugu / Hindi / English) explaining that SETU is a classroom tool that **does not retain children's voice recordings, photographs, or disability labels**.
3. **Voluntary Participation:** Students can choose whether and how they interact with the platform at any time without academic penalty.
4. **No Medical / Diagnostic Claim:** Evaluators and teachers are instructed that SETU is strictly an assistive learning tool, not a diagnostic battery.

---

## 4. Evaluation Methodology & Metrics

```
Phase 1: Pre-Session Baseline (15 mins)
  - Inspect classroom audio acoustics and network connectivity.
  - Brief teacher on starting session and mic positioning.

Phase 2: Live Classroom Observation (40 mins)
  - Deliver standard NCERT/SCERT lesson (e.g. The Solar System).
  - Record:
      * Time taken for all students to join (target < 60s)
      * Caption latency (target < 1.5s)
      * Number of teacher caption corrections
      * Frequency of student AAC card selections
      * Student mode switching patterns

Phase 3: Post-Session Qualitative Feedback (20 mins)
  - Semi-structured interviews with teacher and special educator.
  - Visual Likert scale feedback with students.
```

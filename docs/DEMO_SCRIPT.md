# SETU — 3-Minute SevaFirst Demonstration Script

**Scenario:** Class 6 Science — *The Solar System and Earth's Movements*  
**Teacher:** Anita Sharma  
**Target Time:** 3 Minutes  

---

## Step 1: Teacher Portal Launch (0:00 – 0:30)
1. Open SETU in the browser (`http://127.0.0.1:8000`).
2. Click **Launch Teacher Demo** (or 1-Click Sign In as **Anita Sharma**).
3. On the Teacher Dashboard, select **Class 6 - Section A** and lesson **Class 6 Science — The Solar System**.
4. Click **Start Live Session**.
5. Note the generated **6-character Room Code** (e.g. `SETU89`) and click **Project QR** to display the code on the screen.

---

## Step 2: Student Device Joins (0:30 – 1:00)
1. Open a separate browser tab or mobile device window.
2. Click **Join Classroom** and enter the room code (e.g. `SETU89`).
3. Enter nickname `Arya` and click **Join Live Class**.
4. The student immediately sees the active lesson, live status indicator, and 4 accessible modes.

---

## Step 3: Live Speech Captions & Teacher Correction (1:00 – 1:45)
1. On the Teacher tab, click **Start Live Speech** (or toggle microphone).
2. Speak clearly into the microphone:
   > *"The Earth revolves around the Sun. One complete revolution takes approximately 365 days."*
3. Watch captions stream live onto both the teacher screen and student tab under **Mode 1 (Read)**.
4. On the teacher screen, click the **Pencil Icon** on a caption segment and add:
   > *"The Earth revolves around the Sun in an elliptical orbit."*
5. Click **Save & Broadcast**. Observe how the student's screen immediately updates with the corrected text marked with a `✓ Teacher corrected` badge.

---

## Step 4: Multimodal Listen & Diagram Description (1:45 – 2:15)
1. On the student device, click **Mode 2 (Listen)**.
2. Click **Play Narration** to hear text-to-speech audio of the current section.
3. Scroll to the planetary diagram and click **Diagram Spoken Description**.
4. The system speaks the pedagogical verbal description:
   > *"A central glowing yellow Sun with eight planetary orbits radiating outward..."*

---

## Step 5: Augmentative Communication (AAC) Two-Way Loop (2:15 – 2:45)
1. On the student device, switch to **Mode 3 (Respond)**.
2. Tap the preset card: **"Why do we have day and night?"** (or **"Please repeat that"**).
3. The message is sent. The student sees a green confirmation badge: *"Message Sent! Waiting for teacher..."*
4. Switch to the Teacher tab. The question appears in real time in the **Student Questions & AAC Inbox**.
5. Teacher clicks **Acknowledge** (or types a brief reply).
6. The student screen updates in real time to show **"Teacher acknowledged your message!"**.

---

## Step 6: Step-by-Step Follow & Teacher Announcement (2:45 – 3:00)
1. Teacher advances the lesson section to Section 3 (Earth's Rotation) and clicks **Sync**.
2. Student in **Mode 4 (Follow)** sees the lesson advance seamlessly to the rotation concept card.
3. Teacher clicks **Announcement**, types *"Unit Test Next Monday on Chapters 1-4"*, and clicks **Publish**.
4. The high-contrast announcement banner appears with sound and a **Read Aloud** button on all student screens.
5. Teacher clicks **End Live Class**. The session terminates cleanly.

---

## Key Evaluator Takeaway
**One lesson prepared by the teacher → Four simultaneous accessible pathways (Read, Listen, Respond, Follow) → Instant two-way classroom participation without child profiling.**
